## Caption

TRIPOD ATTACK! is out now. 🛸

Shoot the legs. Duck the Heat-Ray. Protect the people. Take Europe back.

A Vectrex-style vector arcade game after H. G. Wells's The War of the Worlds. The fighting-machines
are real physics: break a leg and the whole thing comes down differently every time, in a shower
of sparks. Ten sectors from Belfast to Athens, each with its own skyline, then an endless mode in
Belfast for the high score. Every sound is synthesised in code, 1980s arcade style.

Windows · Linux · Android. Link in bio.
