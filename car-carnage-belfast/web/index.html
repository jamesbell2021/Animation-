HOW TO PLAY

Every turn is one second of the fight, in five phases. You set your speed change, a steering move for each phase, and what to shoot. Then you hit ACTION. When everyone has, all cars move at once and the second plays out in real time. At the end of the match you can watch the whole thing back as a film, or skip it.

Speed

You can accelerate once per turn, up to your car's acceleration (5, 10 or 15 mph). You can brake 10 mph for free. Braking harder is a manoeuvre with a difficulty: 15 mph is D1, 20 mph is D2, 30 mph is D5, 40 mph is D9 and your tyres take damage. Your speed decides how many inches you move in each phase (the movement chart): at 30 mph you move 1" in phases 1, 3 and 5; at 80 mph you move 2, 1, 2, 1, 2.

Steering

You can make one manoeuvre in each phase you move at least 1". A bend turns the car: D1 for every 15° (a 45° bend is D3, 90° is D6). A drift slides the car a quarter inch sideways without turning (D1). At 20–35 mph you can try a bootlegger reverse (D7) to spin the car round on the spot, or a T-stop to slew sideways and stop fast.

Handling and crashes

Your car has a handling class (HC). Every manoeuvre and every hazard (getting shot, hitting oil, debris, a collision) knocks your handling status down by its difficulty. The faster you go and the worse your status, the more likely you lose control: the orders panel shows safe, roll 4+ or LOSES CONTROL for every move before you commit. Lose control and the crash table decides: a skid, a spinout, a roll, or being vaulted into the air. Status recovers by your HC at the end of each turn.

Shooting

Each weapon fires once per turn at your target, in the first phase it has a clear line of fire within its arc: front guns fire forwards, side guns sideways, turrets anywhere. To hit, roll two dice against the weapon's to-hit number, modified by range (−1 per 4"), target speed, target size, your own manoeuvring and smoke. Damage goes into the armour on the side that was hit; what gets through hits weapons, the power plant and the driver. The driver has three hits. Tyres can be aimed at (−3). Dropped weapons (oil, smoke, paint, mines, spikes) go out behind or beside you in the phase you choose.

Collisions

Ramming does damage by speed (3 dice at 40 mph, 11 dice at 80), scaled by the weight of the car doing the ramming. Head-ons add the speeds together. Walls hit back: weak walls (terraces, shop fronts, bus shelters) can be smashed through, but buildings and the peace wall stop you dead. Blue water drowns anything without off-road suspension. Green pools are fallout: tyres suffer. Striped ramps are jumps: hit one at 20+ mph and you fly 1" for every 10 mph over 20.

Winning

Last car able to move or shoot wins. If the turn limit runs out, most kills wins, then most damage dealt.
