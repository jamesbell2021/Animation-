# Neon Mega Tank 1.1: patch notes and demo

Text ready to paste for the itch.io devlog and the demo upload. Version 1.1.0
(Android `version/code=3`).

## Devlog post

**Title:** Neon Mega Tank 1.1 – shields up, towers down, and a free demo

The crew's tank just got a lot more fun to run.

- **A cannon that answers the stick.** The turret now swings to wherever you point in a
  fraction of a second, a gamepad's right stick aims the guns, and a reticle shows exactly where
  the shell will land. Aim assist locks onto the enemy nearest your aim line and keeps tracking
  it after you let go. Tap fire mid-swing and it fires the moment it lines up.
- **The REPAIR seat is a mini-game now.** Tap ACTION when the needle crosses the green to weld
  the hull; hit the bright middle for a PERFECT and build a combo. **Hold ACTION for an energy
  shield** that bounces shells, rockets, mortar rounds and hornets off the whole tank, until its
  charge runs dry.
- **Four new enemies:** hornet swarms that dive at the hull, mortar teams that lob rounds over
  the rooftops (watch for the red ring), barrier trucks that throw an energy dome over their
  friends (drive inside it!), and attack gunships with rocket salvos. Every third wave from
  wave 6 has a theme: Drone Storm, Armour Column or Mortar Barrage.
- **Buildings come down properly.** Shoot out a base cube and the stack above drops, tips and
  smashes into the street in a shower of neon chunks, glass and dust. Knock out half a
  building's base and the whole thing collapses, squashing anything underneath.
- **Free demo:** 10 minutes of battle per launch, on Windows, Linux and Android.

Created by James Bell (3BL). Code written with Claude (AI).

## Demo upload description

Free demo of Neon Mega Tank: the full game with 10 minutes of battle per launch. Crew one giant
neon tank with up to three friends (or the AI crew), on one PC or on your phones over Wi-Fi.

## itch.io settings for the demo files

1. **Edit game → Uploads:** add the three demo files (or let the workflow push them to the
   `windows-demo`, `linux-demo` and `android-demo` channels).
2. On each demo file, tick **This file is a demo and can be downloaded for free**, and set its
   platform (Windows / Linux / Android).
3. Save. The page then shows a **Download demo** button next to the price.
