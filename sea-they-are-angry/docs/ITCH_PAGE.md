# itch.io page: draft copy

For when there's a page to put it on. Facts are as of v1.1.0 (see `docs/DESIGN.md` and `docs/KIRKHAVEN.md`); change a line here when the game changes.

## Title

Sea They Are Angry

## One-line hook

Keep a North Sea lighthouse burning through the night while the drowned walk up out of the water for the light.

## Short description (the card under the title)

A top-down survival game for one to three keepers, late 1980s. Gather by day, hold the light by night.

## The page

You are a lighthouse keeper on a small rock off the coast of Scotland, in the late 1980s. By day you walk the beaches for what the sea has brought, mend what the night broke, sow kelp in the garden and fill the lamp. At dusk the foghorn sounds and the fog comes in, and things that should have stayed under the water walk up the beaches toward the light. Keep the lamp burning, and hold out until the relief boat comes.

Across a swaying rope bridge is the holm, with the huts, wire and searchlights of a wartime internment camp, a generator that eats diesel, and a wind turbine that keeps the lights strung round the wire. Out in a rowing boat is Gull Rock, where the gulls nest. The creatures raid the camp as well as the light, so a night is two fights and you are never enough people for both. You are always three keepers: whoever isn't played by a person is played by the computer.

The sea has moods. Its anger builds as the nights go on, and the lamp's wide beam slows what comes while its focused beam burns them, at the price of oil. Anything that falls in the water washes ashore at dawn; whatever is left lying at dusk, the sea takes back.

It begins in Kirkhaven, the harbour town where the Board put the light on a clock. The clock stuck, the fog came in against the wind, and something came with it. For three nights you put the town's lamps back one at a time and try to keep seven of its people alive; then you run for the boat with whoever is left, to the one light on the coast still kept by hand. On the rock you arm, because the old camp's diary says what is coming. On the last night it comes.

The nights are made in the spirit of John Carpenter's *The Fog*: the bank rolling in against the wind, shapes in it, a cold synthesiser score, a voice on the wireless, a knock at the door. The story is told in a keeper's log, drawn in pencil.

## Features

- A story in two acts with an ending: the fall of Kirkhaven, the rock, and the kraken.
- Seven named townspeople to keep alive, each of whom helps while they live.
- A fog bank that glows, comes ashore ahead of each landing, and is held off by light.

- Gather, repair, plant and cook by day; defend a lighthouse and a camp by night
- Five kinds of creature, each with its own way in: the Bloated Dead, the wall-crawling Squid, the armoured Crab, the giant Eel and the Smotherer that climbs the tower to put the light out
- A shed's workbench: boathook, longbow, shotgun, a rigged-up flamer and petrol bombs, rat poison for the trap cairns
- A wartime camp to run: a generator and searchlights, crops under glass, a research desk, hunger and cold
- Three islands, a rope bridge and a rowing boat; calm, stormy and gale nights with real lightning
- One to three people on one screen (it splits when you're apart), or each on your own machine over a LAN
- Three seas, calm, angry and raging, from forgiving to hard
- Characters, creatures and animation built in Blender; the music and every sound made in code
- Colour-blind friendly keeper colours, a steadier-lights option, adjustable screen shake and interface size, and every key can be changed

## Controls

Keyboard and mouse: WASD move, mouse aims, left mouse attacks, E uses, R works the lamp, Q switches the beam, F pours dirty fuel, Shift sprints, Space jumps, Tab or 1 to 5 change weapon. Playing alone, hold the middle mouse button and drag (or Z and C) to turn the camera, and use the wheel to zoom. A gamepad works too. Full layout in the game's How to Play and Settings.

## Co-op

Up to three people on one screen: arrow keys and gamepads, or press Insert (arrow keys) or A on a second pad to join a game under way, by day. Or play over a LAN, one keeper to a machine.

## Early access note

This is an early build: Chapter One of a longer story. Chapter Two, The Harbour, is set up at the end of the first but isn't built yet. It has had little play outside the team, so **feedback is very welcome**: press F12 at any moment in the game to save a snapshot of where you are (it goes in `feedback.txt` next to your saves), and tell us what you were doing.
