# Sea They Are Angry: v1.0.0, the whole story

The game now has both its chapters and an ending, and the nights have been remade in the
spirit of John Carpenter's *The Fog*: a glowing bank that rolls in off the sea, dark
shapes in it, a cold synthesiser score, a voice on the wireless, a knock at the door.

Nothing of the film itself is used: the story, the words and the music are the game's own.

## The fog

- **A fog bank that glows.** At dusk a bank rolls in across the water and lies just off
  the shore all night, lit from within. It rolls and breathes, and it is drawn on every
  graphics setting.
- **It comes ashore ahead of them.** Before each landing a tongue of fog pushes up that
  beach, so what wades out of the sea is a dark shape against a bright ground.
- **Light holds it off.** The lamp, the cottage's windows and the camp while its lights
  burn each keep a clearing in it.
- **Darker nights**, so the bank, the beam and the lamps are what you see by.

## What happens in the dark

- **The wireless.** At dusk a transmission comes through: coastguard traffic, the
  harbour office at Kirkhaven, and as the nights go on, less and less that makes sense.
- **The knock.** From the second night, when a keeper is alone indoors: three slow
  knocks at the cottage door. Nothing is there.
- **The lights dip** as the fog comes up a beach.
- **A foghorn that isn't yours,** far out, on a night when no ship is due.
- Each of these has a caption, for anyone playing without sound.

## The score

Seven new pieces, synthesised in code like everything else you hear: cold, slow analogue
synthesiser music for the title, the nights, the last night, losing, the harbour by day
and by night, and the ending. The day on the rock and the end of Chapter One keep their
old airs.

## Chapter Two: The Harbour

The relief boat puts the keepers ashore at Kirkhaven, a fishing town whose lights went
out on a Tuesday. Its people are shut in the kirk. From the title, choose **Chapter Two:
The Harbour**, or go on from the end of Chapter One.

- **A town to relight.** Eleven street lamps along the quay, up the wynds and at the kirk
  gate. Feed the harbour's generator, switch each lamp in at its fuse box, and they burn
  from dusk in sodium orange.
- **The fog is where they are.** It cannot lie in a burning lamp's light. What stands in
  lamplight is slowed and scorched; a landing with a lamp at its head lets fewer through,
  and they would sooner come up a dark one. So they come for the lamps.
- **The harbour light** at the end of the pier is worked like the rock's. It reaches both
  slips but not the strand.
- **The kirk.** Knock, and someone answers through the door. Splice its bell rope, and
  once a night the bell makes every burning lamp flare white.
- **The last night** the fog comes over the quay and into the streets.
- **An ending.** Hold the town to the last dawn and the story is finished.
- Four nights on a calm sea, five on an angry one, six raging. The computer's keepers
  come with you: one on the harbour light, one walking the lamps.
- Chapter Two is for one to three people on one machine. LAN games are Chapter One's.

How it is built, and why, is in [CHAPTER_TWO.md](CHAPTER_TWO.md).
