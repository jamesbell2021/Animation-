# Sea They Are Angry: v0.1.1, polish

A round of polish on v0.1.0 (Chapter One), from a look at the game frame by frame as a player sees it.

## Easier to read

- **A new HUD:**
  - **Top left:** a card with the day or night and its clock, the weather, the sea's mood, and bars for the lighthouse and the lamp's fuel.
  - **Bottom left:** a card for each keeper with health, hunger and warmth as bars.
  - **Top right:** the store as a row of coloured items.
  - **Right:** the first days' jobs in a panel that no longer runs off the screen.
  - **Keys:** shown as you've set them.
- **Markers off screen:** an arrow at the edge of the screen points at creatures out of view, with a count. A raid on the camp while you're at the light shows at once. The Eel and the Crab get a bigger gold arrow.
- **A summary at the end of a run:** how many nights the light held, what came up out of the sea and how it was stopped, and what it cost. You can go straight back to the title from there.
- **Prices say kelp,** not seaweed.

## Better to play

- **A weapon bar** along the bottom of the screen, numbered 1 to 5 for the keys that take each up: the boathook, longbow, shotgun, flamer and petrol bombs, each with what it has left. What's in hand is lit gold, and what you don't have yet is dim. Press the number for one you haven't got and it tells you where to get it.
- **A camera you can move, playing alone** (as in Company of Heroes): hold the middle mouse button and drag, or Z and C, to turn it round the island. The wheel zooms in toward your keeper (never too close) and out again, and X puts it back. Walking follows the camera, so up the screen is always away from it. With the camera free, Tab and 1 to 5 change what's in hand.

- **Hits land:** the screen shakes with blows, bursts, slams, the Eel's surge, the shotgun, petrol bombs and lightning. A heavy blow freezes the world for a few frames, and a creature flashes pale when it's hit. Settings, Display, Screen shake turns the shake off.
- **Cheaper camp repairs:** a driftwood mends a hut by 70. The wire, the generator and the turbine take a scrap, or a driftwood when there's no scrap, and two mends bring each back from wrecked. Before, the camp stayed wrecked from about night 4.
- **Kelp broth** at the cottage: 3 kelp make a meal for the store.
- **The computer's keepers:**
  - **Salvage:** they pick up what creatures leave lying on their island before the sea takes it back.
  - **Food:** they boil eggs or a broth when the store is out of food.

## Better to look at

- **The mist** at dusk and through the night drifts in soft sheets, lit by the lamp's beam, and lies a while at dawn. It used to be dots of fog.
- **Whitecaps** on a calm sea are faint lace, and they grow with the storm. Before, they looked like scraps of paper on the water.
- **The crab's and squid's glowing eyes** now move with their bodies.

## Playing it

The same as v0.1.0: unzip and run `SeaTheyAreAngry.exe` (Windows), or untar and run `SeaTheyAreAngry.x86_64` (Linux).
