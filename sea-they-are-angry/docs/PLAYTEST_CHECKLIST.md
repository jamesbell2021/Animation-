# Playtest checklist

Most of what's below has passed the automated tests but hasn't yet been played by a person. A test proves that something works; only playing shows whether it's fun, readable and fair. Play with this list beside you and note anything that feels wrong, even if you can't say why.

Start from the title screen with **New Run**. One keeper is enough for most of it; the co-op and LAN parts need a second pad or a second machine.

## Before you start

- [ ] **Settings** (title screen): set the volume, try full screen and a window, and pick the graphics. On a laptop, does Low (or a render scale of 77% or 85%) make it smooth? Does it still look like the game?
- [ ] **Change a key**, for example Use from E to G. Do the prompts in the game now say [G]? Does putting Use on R swap it with the lamp's key?
- [ ] **A gamepad**, playing alone: does it work as keeper one straight away? Does the right stick aim, and does moving the mouse take aim back?

## The first three days

- [ ] **The jobs list** on the right: do the jobs make sense in that order, and does each tick off when you do it? Is anything on it confusing, or anything missing that a new player needs to know?
- [ ] **Too much at once?** Note anything you didn't understand on the first day: what a resource is for, where to go, what the HUD's numbers mean.
- [ ] **The rope bridge** to the holm: does crossing it feel good? Does the screen split sensibly when keepers are apart (co-op only)?

## The holm and the camp

- [ ] **Does the holm look like a real islet?** The coves at the beaches, the cliffs, the shelf at their foot, the rocks in the water, the turf and the outcrops. Anything that looks odd from the game's camera: floating rocks, sea showing through the land, seams?
- [ ] **Does the camp look like an old wartime camp?** The huts, the towers, the generator house, the sandbags and the rest. Is anything too dark, too clean, or out of place?
- [ ] **Can you walk everywhere you'd expect,** and does nothing in the camp snag the keeper?

## The wind turbine and the camp's lights

- [ ] **Does it look right** for the 1980s, on its knoll by the camp? Do the lights round the wire make the camp at night feel better?
- [ ] **When it's attacked:** do you notice the lights blink, and know why? When it's wrecked and the camp goes dark, do you want to go and mend it?

## Gull Rock

- [ ] **Did you notice it** from the main island (top of the screen), and want to go?
- [ ] **The boat:** is it clear where to get in (the east beach's jetty)? Does the crossing feel good, or too long? Does the rowing look right? Try two keepers in the boat together, and hauling it over on its line from the rock.
- [ ] **The eggs:** worth the trip? Is the gulls' mobbing fun, or annoying?
- [ ] **The gulls:** do they make the place feel alive? Too many, too few, too loud? Do they go at dusk and stay away in a storm?

## The fights

- [ ] **The shed's weapons:** the shotgun (does 10 cartridges feel too few, or about right?), the flamer, the longbow, petrol bombs and rat poison at a trap cairn.
- [ ] **Helpers with weapons:** the lighthouse's helper takes the shotgun if nobody has it. Does it fire at the right things, at a sensible range? Does it waste cartridges? Make a longbow for a helper at the workbench: does it shoot well?
- [ ] **Raids on the camp:** from night 2. Are they fair? Can you tell where they've landed?

## The end of Chapter One

- [ ] **The countdown:** is "boat in N nights" easy to see? Does it make you want to keep going?
- [ ] **The last night:** does it feel like a final push? Too hard, or not hard enough?
- [ ] **The boat:** does the *Mary Ann* look right coming in? Is it obvious that everyone has to go down and get aboard?
- [ ] **The chapter card:** does the text read well? Does it make you want Chapter Two? Try both "Back to the title" and "Stay on the rock".
- [ ] **The length:** 10 nights on the normal sea. Too long, too short? (A calm sea is 8, a raging one 12, and mending the camp's wireless brings the boat two nights sooner.)

## The three seas

- [ ] **Calm:** easy enough for someone new to finish? Ammunition never runs out there.
- [ ] **Angry:** the game as it's meant. Does it get harder at the right rate?
- [ ] **Raging:** hard but fair?

## Added since v0.1.0 (v0.1.1 to v0.1.4)

Everything here has passed the tests but not a person.

- [ ] **The music.** Does the title's air, the day's piece, the night's, and the last night's reel fit what's happening? Too loud, too long, too repetitive? Does the sound level in Settings reach the music?
- [ ] **The HUD.** The night card top left, the keepers' cards bottom left, the store top right, the weapon bar along the bottom. Can you read it all at a glance, in a storm and at night? Does anything get in the way of the play?
- [ ] **The camera, playing alone:** hold the middle mouse button and drag, or Z and C, to turn it; the wheel or = and - to zoom; X to put it back. Does the turn feel right? Does it come in close enough, and not too close? Do W, A, S, D follow the camera however it's turned?
- [ ] **The weapon bar and 1 to 5.** Does the number take the weapon up? What does it say when you press the number for one you don't have yet?
- [ ] **Walk into the cottage, the shed and the huts.** Does the roof lift only when you're clearly inside, and not flicker at the door? Is the room worth seeing? With two people playing, does the keeper's outline show through the roof?
- [ ] **A jump (Space), and walking into a fence to climb it.** Can you get back up from the beach? Does the climb feel like a move, or like a glitch?
- [ ] **The outline.** Standing behind the lighthouse or a tower, can you still see where your keeper is?
- [ ] **Hits.** The screen shake, the pause on a heavy blow, the pale flash on a creature, the numbers floating up. Too much, too little? Try Settings, Comfort and access, and turn each down.
- [ ] **The arrows at the edge of the screen** pointing at creatures you can't see: do they help, and do they tell you the right thing about a raid on the camp?
- [ ] **Salvage.** Creatures leave bio, scrap or kelp where they fall. Do you pick it up in time? Does the sea taking what's left at dusk feel fair?
- [ ] **Gull Rock.** Row out by day, take the eggs, row back before dark. Do the keeper's hands stay on the oars? Do the gulls go at night and in a storm?
- [ ] **The wind turbine** on the holm and the lights round the camp: do they go out when it's wrecked? Does the turbine turn faster in a squall?
- [ ] **Kelp broth** at the cottage, and the camp's cheaper mends: is there ever too little scrap, or too much kelp?
- [ ] **Join a game under way** (by day): press Insert for the arrow keys' keeper, or A on a second pad. Does it seat the newcomer sensibly, and does the screen split when you're apart?
- [ ] **The end of a run:** the summary of the night and what came, and Try again / Back to the title.
- [ ] **Comfort and access:** Reduce flashing (a steady lamp and a soft lightning glow), the other keeper colours, the interface size 125% and 150%, and the shake Off, Low and Full. Do they all hold up on every screen?
- [ ] **F12** saves a snapshot to `feedback.txt` and says so. Use it the moment something feels off, and say what you were doing when you report.
- [ ] **The local play log** (Settings, Comfort and access, or start with `--telemetry`): after a run, is `telemetry.jsonl` filled in? See `docs/TELEMETRY.md` for where.

## Co-op and the LAN

- [ ] **Two keepers on one screen** with a pad: the split screen, reviving a downed keeper, sharing the shed.
- [ ] **On the LAN** (two machines, Play on the LAN on both): does it feel smooth, or does anything stutter or jump? Do the squid's ink and the thrown boathook show on the second machine? What happens when one machine leaves?

## The Windows build

- [ ] Run `SeaTheyAreAngry.exe` from the Windows build (keep the `.pck` beside it). Does it start, look right and play smoothly?

## Reporting

For each problem, note the night, which island, what you were doing, and what you expected. A screenshot, or a short video on a phone, helps a lot.
