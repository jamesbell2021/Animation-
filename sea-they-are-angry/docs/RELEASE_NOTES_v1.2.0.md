# Sea They Are Angry: v1.2.0, The Escape

## The Escape: a new level between the town and the rock

On the third night the *Mary Ann* leaves Kirkhaven's pier with whoever you got aboard
and runs up the coast to the rock in the fog. They come up out of the water after her.

- **Someone steers.** Take the wheel behind the wheelhouse ([F]) and steer with your move
  keys through the rocks, and away from the great arms that rear up beside her course
  and come down on the red ring where she's headed. If you don't, a helper steers; with no
  helpers, Dod Wiseman does, though not as well. You can take the wheel from either.
- **Everyone else keeps them off.** The drowned and the bloated swim up and climb her hull;
  a **!** over the rail marks each one. Any hit (the boathook, an arrow, the shotgun, fire)
  knocks a climber back into the sea. Those that get aboard take Kirkhaven's people over
  the side, and a bloated one bursts among them. Arrows, shot or a bomb drive a rearing arm
  back under.
- **Her hull** takes every rock and arm. If it goes she founders, and Continue starts the
  crossing again.
- **The light.** When the rock's light reaches her they fall back, and she comes in under it.
- About three and a half minutes. Harder on an angry sea, harder still raging.

## The screen

- **No briefing box at the start.** The card over the middle of the screen when a run
  began is one line at the bottom now; the jobs list says the rest.
- **Fold any panel with a click, at any time.** Click a panel's heading (the ▾ at its end)
  to shrink it to one line, and again to open it. No need to pause, and your keeper doesn't
  swing for the click.

## Not yet judged by a person

- The Escape has been played only by the computer's keepers: how hard it is, whether the
  arms give enough warning, and how it feels to steer.
