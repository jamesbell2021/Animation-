# Sea They Are Angry: v1.3.0, making it great

## Hits that land

- A solid blow from your keeper freezes the moment for a few hundredths of a second, kicks
  the camera toward it, flashes the creature white, lands with a thunk and sparks, and
  shoves it back. A full-draw arrow or a point-blank barrel knocks them reeling; kills fling
  the body. (The pause and the kick follow the hit-pause and shake settings.)
- The swing carries your keeper a short step into it.

## Each keeper has a knack

- **Callum** hits hardest with the boathook, and knocks climbers off the ledge more surely.
- **Hamish** is the shot: a tighter shotgun spread with an extra pellet, and a quicker draw.
- **Mairi** mends and refuels faster, and her mends cost less.
- **Ewan** is quickest on his feet and up again soonest (he joins when a fourth keeper can).

## The rock's days and nights differ

- **Each day brings something:** a wreck on a beach with stores to fetch before dusk, old
  camp kit, fish, a long day, the gulls' nests full, a page washed up, something on the
  wireless, a low tide that opens a cave. A small note at the top says what, and a gold
  marker shows where.
- **Some nights are special,** and the morning gives a sign: a storm, a squid night, fog
  with landings on every beach, smotherers for the lamp, the eel, and a big night every
  third.
- **The seven ask for things.** Each survivor asks one thing on some day (driftwood, eggs,
  kelp, the minister's bible from a wreck, the stove lit...) and repays it in their own way:
  more at dawn, a box of cartridges, the generator's governor, the nurse's dressings. When
  one of them is lost, the others grieve at dawn.
- **A shorter, fuller rock:** six nights on a calm sea, seven angry, eight raging, with the
  kraken on the last.

## The kraken fights in three stages

1. **It takes the camp.** Its arms come over the wire one at a time. Fire, a couple of good
   arrows, or a searchlight held on an arm drive it back.
2. **It comes round for the light.** An arm that slams down on land lies there with a pale
   wound: cut it then, and it stops and slows.
3. **The light.** Blind it with the focused beam and hit it while it's blind; each blinding
   is shorter. Leave it looking and it grips the tower. At the last it rears its whole body
   out of the sea.

A strip at the top shows the stage and its strength.

## Stars and unlocks

- Each dawn rates the night with up to three stars: the light kept, nobody lost, nothing
  through. The run's total is on the end card.
- Finishing the story on an angry sea unlocks a yellow oilskin for your keepers; on a raging
  sea, Hard Light (the lamp burns oil faster, for a fourth star). Turn them on in Settings.

## Not yet judged by a person

- How the hits feel (the pauses may be too much or too little), the kraken's three stages,
  the day events and requests on screen, and how hard the shorter rock is. All of it has been
  played only by the computer's keepers and the tests.
- The helpers never use petrol bombs or the flamer on the kraken, so playing with fire
  yourself should make that fight shorter.
