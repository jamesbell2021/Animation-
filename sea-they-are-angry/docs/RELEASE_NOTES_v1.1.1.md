# Sea They Are Angry: v1.1.1, heard and seen

What the owner found playing v1.1.0, put right, and the three looks fixes promised
after it.

## Keys that have moved

- **Q and E turn the camera**, all the way round, when you play alone (they were Z and
  C, and nobody found them). X puts it back, the wheel zooms, the middle mouse button
  dragged turns it too. A line on screen says so a few seconds into a run.
- To free those keys: **Use is F** (it was E), **the beam is B** (it was Q), **dirty
  fuel and the kirk bell are G** (they were F). Every prompt in the game names the new
  keys, and all of them can be changed in Settings.

## Sound

- **The music can be heard.** It was playing all along, but measured in the game's own
  mix it sat 5 to 9 dB under the sea and the wind. The score is louder, the fair-weather
  sea and wind quieter, and a limiter keeps a loud night from clipping. Measured after:
  the score about 4 dB over the sea by day, level with a busy night.
- **The title theme has a pulse**: the nights' steady synthesiser bass under the piano
  figure, so it is a theme and not a wash of chords.
- **Recorded sounds** in place of the ones made in code, where a free recording could
  be found: see below, and [SOUND_CREDITS.md](SOUND_CREDITS.md) for where each came
  from. Only public-domain and CC0 recordings are used.

Recordings now in the game (twenty):

- **The weather:** the sea (surf on a shore), the wind (Cape Finisterre), the rain, a
  wave breaking.
- **The birds:** three herring gull cries.
- **Bells and horns:** the lighthouse's foghorn (a real diaphone, East Brother Island
  Light), the *Mary Ann*'s horn, the kirk bell, the buoy bell, the camp's hand bell.
- **Things done:** the shotgun, the hammer, glass breaking, a splash, a footstep on
  gravel, the knock at the door, the camp's diesel generator.

Still made in code: the thunder, every creature and the kraken, fire, the wireless, and
the small interface sounds. The thunder was recorded too and then taken back out: its
source says it is public domain, but it was uploaded by someone other than the person
who recorded it, and that was not certain enough to ship.

## The title

- The island turns once in eighty seconds, not twenty.

## Looks

- **The seven of Kirkhaven look like themselves.** They borrowed the keepers' models
  unchanged; now the minister is in black with his collar, the chandler in a brown coat
  and headscarf, the engineer in a boiler suit, the publican in her apron, the nurse in
  cape and cap, the harbour master in navy with a peaked cap, the net-mender in a cream
  jumper.
- **Kirkhaven is paved.** Granite setts on the quay and streets, flagged pavements with
  kerbs, dressed blocks on the pier, worn and stained patches, puddles, drains, mooring
  rings, steps up to the kirk, weeds at the wall feet, damp rising up the harled walls,
  a strand with a wrack line. Looks only: nothing about where you can walk has changed.
- **The keeper's log.** The kraken is drawn into the diary pages and the morning after.
  The page for the third night is redrawn (the creatures coming over the quay wall, the
  harbour light dead behind them), the people at the boat wear their own clothes, and
  the six pages before Act One are drawn again over the paved town.

## Not yet judged by a person

- **Everything you hear.** The mix was set by measurement, the title theme changed, and
  the recordings were chosen and levelled from their licences, pictures of their sound
  and their measured loudness, without being listened to. Some will be wrong: the kirk
  bell is a small bell slowed down, the generator a diesel mower slowed down, the surf
  is a lake's. Say which, and each can be swapped on its own.
- **The Windows build** has still never been run on Windows.
