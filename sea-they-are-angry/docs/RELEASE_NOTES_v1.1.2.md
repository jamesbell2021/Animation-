# Sea They Are Angry: v1.1.2, what the owner found

Fixes for what the owner found playing v1.1.1.

- **No more gold patches on the keeper.** The outline that shows your keeper through a
  wall or the lighthouse was lighting parts of his own body (boot tops, belt, shoulders)
  in his colour, out in the open. It now comes on only when something really stands
  between the camera and the keeper.
- **A helper hands over what you want.** Playing alone, go to the lamp while a helper is
  on it and the helper gives it to you and comes out. The same goes for a camp searchlight
  ("Take over the searchlight from Hamish") and for the shotgun or the flamer when you press
  its number.
- **Fire that belongs in the scene.** Burning creatures, petrol-bomb fires and the flamer
  are smooth, licking flames now, not pixel blocks.
- **A proper longbow:** one smooth stave with horn tips and a leather grip in the hand, and
  a string that comes back as you draw. It was a broken chain of planks.
- **A louder shotgun:** about 8 dB louder on average.
- **Shooting no longer knocks you over.** The shotgun's recoil threw the keeper into a
  ragdoll on every shot. It's a push of a step now.
- **Ragdolls stay in one piece.** Feet, shins and hands could whirl wildly when a body went
  down, sometimes flinging it apart. They're heavier and damped, and a body that starts to
  fly apart is stilled at once.
