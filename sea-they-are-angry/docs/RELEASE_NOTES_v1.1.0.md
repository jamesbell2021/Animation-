# Sea They Are Angry: v1.1.0, the story turned round

The owner's changes to v1.0.0: the town comes first, there are people in it to keep
alive, the story ends with a kraken, the story is told in a keeper's log drawn in
pencil, and the screen is less crowded. [STORY.md](STORY.md) is the story as it now is.

## The story, in its new order

- **Act One: Kirkhaven.** New Run starts here. The Board put the harbour light on a
  clock, the clock stuck, and the fog came in with something in it. You have three
  nights. The town cannot be held: on the third, the *Mary Ann* sounds her horn and you
  bring whoever is left down through the streets to the boat.
- **Act Two: the rock.** The survivors shelter in the old camp. Nobody is coming to take
  you off; the days are for arming, because the camp's diary says what is coming.
- **The last night: the kraken.**

## The seven of Kirkhaven

Seven named people (the minister, the chandler, the engineer, the publican, the nurse,
the harbour master, the net-mender) are in the town and can be lost.

- By day they are about their own doors. Before dusk they shut themselves in the kirk.
- If the kirk doors are broken down in the night, those inside are taken one by one
  until the doors are mended.
- On the last night they have to cross the town on foot to the boat. Out of doors in the
  dark they are prey: creatures turn aside for them, and anything that gets within reach
  of one takes them. Every lamp you kept burning is part of a lit road for them.
- Each helps while they live, in the town and on the rock: scrap, a meal, cartridges and
  arrows each dawn; the generator topped up each dusk; the keepers healed each dawn; the
  camp's bell rung when something lands on the holm.
- On the rock they live in the camp. A hut wrecked in the night can cost one of them. On
  the last night they stand with the keepers on the crag and loose what they have at it.

## The kraken

- A sea titan in the manner of the great stop-motion monsters: it rears upright out of
  the sea and moves in held, stepped poses, twelve to the second. It is the game's own
  creature; no film's design is copied.
- It rises off the east of the holm on the last night and tears the camp down, from the
  east and then from the north.
- Then it comes round to the east beach for the lighthouse.
- An arm rears before it comes down: that is the warning. An arm lying ashore can be cut
  back, which hurts it.
- **The focused beam held in its eyes blinds it.** Blind, it cannot strike, its arms drop,
  and everything that hits it counts for two and a half times as much.
- When it dies the fog goes out, the storm ends, and the camera looks up from the
  keepers to the lighthouse against a sky clearing blue. Then the last pages of the log,
  and the credits.
- **The credits' music** is an arrangement of the traditional Skye Boat Song. Flower of
  Scotland was asked for, but it is in copyright (Roy Williamson, 1967) and cannot be
  shipped without a licence; `audio/music_credits.ogg` is the one file to replace if a
  licence is had.

## The keeper's log

The story is told in pages of a logbook: a pencil drawing and a line or two of the head
keeper's entry. Before Act One, on the crossing to the rock, when the diary's last pages
are read, and after the end. The drawings are made from the game's own scenes by a
sketch shader; nobody drew them by hand.

## A quieter screen

- **One key (H) cycles the display: Full, Slim, Off.**
- In Full, panels shrink themselves: the jobs list folds to a line once you are under
  way, helpers' cards are a name and a health bar, the fed and warm bars show only when
  low, and the store hides what you have none of.
- When the mouse is free (paused, or in a menu), click a panel's header to fold it.

## Also changed

- The title's **New Run** starts the story in Kirkhaven. **Start on the Rock** skips the
  town (all seven saved). LAN games are the rock alone and keep the relief boat's ending.
- Kirkhaven is three nights on every sea; the rock in the story is 6, 8 or 10.
- The ending in which the town was relit (v1.0.0's Chapter Two) is gone.

## Not yet judged by a person

- Everything in v1.0.0's list still stands: the music and sounds unheard, the darker
  nights, the Windows build.
- **The kraken fight** has been run by scripts and by the computer's keepers, not played.
- **The evacuation** likewise.
- **The pencil pages** are a shader's drawings; whether they read as hand-drawn is for
  the owner to say.
