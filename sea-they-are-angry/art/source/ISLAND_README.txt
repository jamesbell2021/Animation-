SEA THEY ARE ANGRY — ISLAND ENVIRONMENT BLOCKOUT

File: island_environment.glb
Format: glTF Binary (GLB), suitable for importing into Godot or Blender.

Contains a single assembled low-poly island environment: irregular rocky cliffs, grassy plateau, striped lighthouse with lantern, three small coastal buildings, fenced garden plots, dock and rowboat, storage props, lamps, stones and sparse coastal grass. Materials are simple vertex-independent PBR base colours.

Scale is approximate (island about 27 x 19 units; lighthouse about 9 units tall). Origin is near the centre of the island at sea level. Z is up.

This is a playable-environment blockout, not a final optimized production asset. It does not include animated sea, dynamic fog, lighting setup, collision meshes, navigation, or separate gameplay scripts. Import into Godot, add a StaticBody3D/concave collision for the terrain as appropriate, and build water/fog as separate scene elements.
