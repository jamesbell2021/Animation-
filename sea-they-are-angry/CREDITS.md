# Credits

**Sea They Are Angry**, a 3BL_Indie game.

Made by James, Aaron, Connor and Bonnie.

- Fonts: IM FELL English by Igino Marini (SIL OFL 1.1) and Special Elite by Astigmatic (Apache 2.0). See `fonts/CREDITS.md`.
- The music and most of the sounds are synthesised in code (`art/source/music/build_music.py`, `autoload/audio.gd`). Twenty of the sounds (the sea, the wind, the rain, the gulls, the bells, the foghorn and others) are recordings, every one CC0 or public domain: each is listed with its source and licence in [docs/SOUND_CREDITS.md](docs/SOUND_CREDITS.md).
- Made with Godot Engine (godotengine.org/license).

The in-game credits screen (title menu → Credits) and the 3BL_Indie splash live in `studio/`.
