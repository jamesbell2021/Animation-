# Lookee Lookee Tycoon

**Hello chicken nugget!**

You're a beach vendor on the Spanish Costa with one blanket, a bag of plastic junk and a voice
that can carry across three hotels. Shout "Hello chicken nugget!", sell Ray-Bams and inflatable
flamingos to sunburnt tourists, and say "Very good very nice!" every time. Then pull the rope on
your blanket the moment the Policía Local turn up.

## Features

- **Make your vendor.** Any skin tone, 13 hairstyles, faces, beards, body types, outfits, gold
  chains, an arm full of watches, sombreros... and your own voice pitch.
- **Shout to sell.** Tourists come in seven kinds (stag parties, pensioners, influencers, yacht
  folk...), each with their own moods, budgets and taste in junk. Some of them haggle.
- **Watch the Heat.** Pack up before the police reach you, or try a bribe, pay the fine, or
  LEG IT across the sand.
- **Build an empire.** Upgrade from a blanket to a pop-up kiosk, hire Shouters, Lookouts, Runners
  and Blanket Sellers, and expand across six resorts from Benidorm to Marbella.
- **Random chaos.** Cruise ships, rain, heatwaves, a seagull with a criminal record and a rival
  vendor you have to out-shout.

Plays in your browser on desktop and phone (landscape), with an Android version to download.

## Controls

Tap or click the sand to walk. Tap a tourist to pitch to them. Drag to look around, and pinch or
scroll to zoom. The big buttons do the rest. Keyboard: Space shouts, E sets up or packs up, B
opens the shop, M changes prices, Esc pauses.
