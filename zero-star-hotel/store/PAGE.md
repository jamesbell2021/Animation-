# Zero Star Hotel

**From the people who brought you Lookee Lookee Tycoon.**

You've bought the worst hotel on the Costa. It has zero stars, two rooms you can actually sleep
in, a cockroach family in room 101, a pool that is mostly algae and a "bar" that is a fridge of
cans. The lads on tour have already checked in. Somebody has already ordered the €1 pints.

Keep your guests (mostly) happy, keep the cockroaches down, stop anybody drowning, and drag the
place up to **three stars**.

## Features

- **Drunken lads, hen parties, backpackers and mums on a getaway.** Every group has its own
  moods, budget and idea of a good night. Win stars and families, couples and pensioners start
  booking. They pay more, drink less and complain about everything.
- **Trouble, all day and all night.** 3am raves, punch-ups at the bar, tellies out of windows,
  thieves in the corridors, belly-flops in the deep end. Tap the warning bubble before the fuse
  runs out and send your manager in.
- **Sunlounger wars.** The mums are up at dawn with their towels. When the loungers run out,
  nobody is backing down.
- **Cockroaches.** Dirty rooms breed them overnight. Tap to squash. Very satisfying.
- **Cheap pints, big consequences.** €1 pints pack the bar, and a packed bar gets rowdy. Set
  your room and pint prices and live with the results.
- **Swimming pool safety.** Clean the swamp, put up a fence and NO DIVING signs, hire a
  lifeguard. Or don't, and find out.
- **Staff.** Cleaners, a handyman, security, a lifeguard and a barman who all get on with it.
- **From crap to less crap.** Open boarded-up rooms and asbestos floors, upgrade rooms from Grim
  to Nice, build a proper bar, a pool slide, karaoke, soundproofing and CCTV.
- **The star inspector.** Meet the checklist, book a visit, and keep the chaos down while the
  clipboard comes round. Three strikes and you fail. Every guest leaves a TrotAdvisor review.

Plays in your browser on desktop and phone (turn it sideways), with an Android version to
download. Your hotel saves automatically.

## How to play

- Tap a **warning bubble** (or its alert on the right) to send your manager.
- Tap a **cockroach** to squash it.
- Tap a **room** to see who's staying and to clean, repair, open or upgrade it.
- **Rooms / Pints** buttons (bottom left) change your prices.
- **UPGRADES** opens the shop: rooms, hotel upgrades, staff, and the Stars checklist.
- Drag to look around; pinch or scroll to zoom. Esc or the pause button pauses.
