ZERO STAR HOTEL - from zero stars to three

Tap a warning bubble: send the manager to sort it out
Tap a cockroach: squash it
Tap a room: see the guests; clean, repair, open or upgrade it
Rooms / Pints buttons: change your prices (cheap pints = rowdy bar)
UPGRADES: rooms, hotel upgrades, staff, and the Stars checklist
Drag: look around        Pinch / mouse wheel: zoom
Esc / II: pause

Meet everything on the Stars checklist, then book the inspector.
Three strikes on inspection day and you fail. Good luck!
