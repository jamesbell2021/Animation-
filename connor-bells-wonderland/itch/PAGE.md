### 🎪 Designed by Connor, age 13. His first game!

Welcome to **Connor's Carnival Chaos**: a mad neon fairground at night, with Cave
Hill and the Harland & Wolff cranes glowing on the Belfast skyline. Four games in
one, up to 8 players, and every winner gets a ridiculous prize.

**🏁 Fairground Race**: six crazy fairground sections, from the Big Top Entrance to
Connor's Crown on top of the Grand Finale Tower. Grab the ? boxes for Catapults,
Mega Bombs, Custard Pies, a giant Plastic Sword, Rocket Boots and more, and use
them on everyone.

**🗼 Tower Climb**: an extreme obby up a 120 m neon tower: stepping stones,
spinning sweepers, vanishing tiles, bounce pads and pushers, with no walls. Watch
out for the **giant Belfast seagull**: it dives at whoever's winning, knocks them
flying, nicks their item and poos on people. The first to the top gets a
fireworks show.

**🎨 Paintball Battle**: last one standing wins **a year's Fruit of the Month
subscription**. Every hit splats you in paint and sends you flying.

**🚗 Dodgem Derby**: ram everyone off the pier into the Irish Sea while the rink
sinks under you, all to pumping hardstyle.

**🏆 Win stupid prizes**: a brand new toaster, a wheelie bin cover, a pet
giraffe called Tower, a pedalo to cross the Irish Sea...

#### Controls

| | |
|---|---|
| Move | WASD or arrow keys |
| Jump (boost in Dodgem Derby) | Space |
| Use item / fire paint | E or F (hold for the Catapult) |
| Menu | MENU button |

#### Play with your mates

Download the **Windows** or **Linux** version and play together on the same Wi-Fi:
one person presses **Host Wi-Fi Game**, everyone else types the IP address it
shows and presses **Join**. Windows and Linux can play together. (On Windows, click
**Allow** when the firewall asks.) The browser version is solo against bots.
