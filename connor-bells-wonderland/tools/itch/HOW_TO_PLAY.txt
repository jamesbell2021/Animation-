CONNOR'S CARNIVAL CHAOS
Designed by Connor, age 13 - his first game!
A 3BL_Indie game, made by James, Aaron, Connor and Bonnie.

STARTING THE GAME
  Windows: double-click ConnorsCarnivalChaos.exe. If Windows says "Windows
           protected your PC", click "More info" then "Run anyway" (the game
           isn't signed, that's all).
  Linux:   double-click ConnorsCarnivalChaos.x86_64, or run
           ./ConnorsCarnivalChaos.x86_64 in a terminal.

CONTROLS
  Move .................. WASD or arrow keys
  Jump .................. Space (boost in Dodgem Derby)
  Use item / fire paint . E or F (hold for the Catapult)
  Menu .................. MENU button

PLAYING WITH FRIENDS (same Wi-Fi)
  1. One person picks a mode and presses "Host Wi-Fi Game". The lobby
     shows their IP address (like 192.168.1.20).
  2. Everyone else types that IP address into the box and presses "Join".
  3. The host presses "Start Race".
  Windows and Linux players can play together. Everyone needs the same
  version of the game. On Windows, click "Allow" when the firewall asks
  (tick "Private networks"). School and public Wi-Fi often block it, so
  play at home.
